description: "Is Jev just a smarter if-statement? That's the question everyone's asking about TypeSafe AI's new \"System One\" model. In this episode, we dig into what Jev actually is: a model that makes fast, typed decisions your code can use directly — and why it points to a bigger shift: stop defaulting to one giant LLM for everything.

Romain sits down with Mike Chambers, Senior Developer Advocate for Generative AI at AWS, to discuss Jev, decision models, and how they complement the LLMs we already know.

Key topics covered:
  • What Jev is, and why it is not just another LLM
  • The three primitives: Noul, Choice, and Score
  • Putting gated decision points inside an agent with Strands
  • Fast model routing, and why cost and speed are the real win
  • Why the right tool for the right job is back
